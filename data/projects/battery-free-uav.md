## Overview

We demonstrated the first flight of a battery-free, energy-harvesting, small-scale
fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicle. The aircraft carries no battery at all: it
flies on solar energy as it is harvested, which makes available power a moving
target set by conditions rather than a reserve the vehicle can draw down.

## Technical Details

- Achieved sustained flight on harvested solar power alone, with no onboard
  energy storage to fall back on
- Designed the aircraft to be *energy-aware*: with no battery buffering supply
  against demand, flight and compute have to track instantaneous harvested power
  directly
- Explored bio-inspired flight strategies, studying how birds exploit soaring to
  stay aloft on limited energy and what that implies for a batteryless airframe
  (DroNet '23)
- Published in Nature Scientific Reports; the underlying approach is covered by
  granted patent US20230391479A1, with a follow-on filing submitted in 2025
