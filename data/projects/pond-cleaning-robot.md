## Overview

Worked in a team to create an autonomous robot capable of scooping trash out of ponds.

## Technical Details

- Developed a Kalman filtering algorithm for accurate localization by fusing IMU and GPS data
- Designed and built custom power and control electronics to manage motors, microcontrollers, and sensors
